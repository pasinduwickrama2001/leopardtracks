What is the Discoveryala Wildlife Journal?

The Discoveryala Wildlife Journal is an authoritative field publication authored by resident senior naturalists and safari trackers operating in Yala National Park, Sri Lanka. Covering Panthera pardus kotiya tracking logs, seasonal elephant corridors, official DWC entrance ticket guides, and park block analyses (Block 1 Ruhuna, Block 5, and Bundala wetlands), every article provides actionable, fact-checked advice for international wildlife travelers.
