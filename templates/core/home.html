ABOUT YALA LEOPARD TRACKS

Yala National Park is Sri Lanka's premier wildlife destination, globally renowned for hosting the highest density of leopards on Earth. At Yala Leopard Tracks, we specialize in bespoke 4x4 jeep safaris and luxury tented camp stays.

Our team of certified senior naturalists and local trackers guide you through Block 1, Block 5, and quiet wilderness buffer zones. We provide customized open-top safari jeeps designed for optimal wildlife photography and viewing comfort.

From morning leopard tracking to afternoon elephant gatherings at waterholes, Yala Leopard Tracks delivers an unforgettable, ethical, and authentic safari adventure.
