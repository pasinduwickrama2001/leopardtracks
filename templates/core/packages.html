Quick Answer for Travelers

What is a Yala Safari Package and What Does It Include?

A Yala safari package with Discoveryala provides private or shared 4x4 safari jeep game drives into Yala National Park (Block 1 Ruhuna or Block 5) with a certified local driver-tracker. Available in morning (05:30 AM – 10:30 AM), afternoon (02:30 PM – 06:00 PM), or full-day (05:30 AM – 06:00 PM) formats, packages range from $55 to $120 USD per private jeep (up to 7 guests) or from $14 USD per person for shared tours. Complimentary hotel pickup is included across Tissamaharama, Palatupana, and Kataragama.

Frequently Asked Questions About Yala Safaris

A private 4x4 safari jeep in Yala costs between $55 and $120 USD per vehicle (accommodating up to 6–7 passengers), depending on duration (4-hour morning, 4-hour evening, or 12-hour full day). Shared budget safari tours start from $14 USD per person. Official DWC entrance tickets are approximately $30–$46 USD per adult depending on group size and current VAT.
Morning game drives depart hotel pickups at 05:00 AM to queue at Palatupana or Katagamuwa gate before official entry at 06:00 AM. Afternoon safaris enter the park at 02:30 PM and exit at 06:00 PM. Full-day expeditions operate from 06:00 AM until 06:00 PM with a designated midday rest inside the park.
Standard modified 4x4 safari jeeps accommodate up to 6 to 7 adult passengers with individual tiered forward-facing seats, open viewing sides, and cushioned roll cages.
Both offer excellent wildlife opportunities. Morning safaris (06:00 AM – 10:00 AM) are optimal for seeing leopards stalking in the cool morning air and active birds. Afternoon safaris (02:30 PM – 06:00 PM) offer dramatic golden-hour photography and elephants gathering near waterholes.
Discoveryala provides door-to-door hotel and villa pickups across Tissamaharama, Kirinda, Palatupana, Kataragama, and Weerawila at no additional fee.
