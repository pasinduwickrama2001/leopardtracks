ABOUT YALA LEOPARD TRACKS

Operating with a strict "Nature First" policy, we bridge luxury and raw wilderness to ensure every safari supports local conservation and ethical wildlife tracking.

OUR HERITAGE & MISSION

15+ YEARS OF ETHICAL YALA WILDLIFE EXPEDITIONS

Founded by local Southern Sri Lankan wildlife conservationists, Yala Leopard Tracks was built on a single core promise: to provide intimate, luxury safari encounters while protecting Yala National Park's fragile ecosystem.

As Sri Lanka's premier eco-expedition partner, we employ exclusively licensed senior naturalist trackers born and raised in the Yala buffer zones. Their generational knowledge of leopard territories, elephant migration paths, and bird nesting grounds ensures unmatched safari experiences.
